Project 1 · Method

Observed vs expected, hospital by hospital.

Patient risk

p = P(death) from age, admission and severity

→

Expected deaths

E = Σ p over each hospital’s patients

→

O/E ratio

Above 1: more deaths than its patients explain

Outcome unknown, dropped: transfers out and patients who left against advice.

Only what care can’t change: stay length and procedures stay out.

Folds split by hospital: no hospital scored by a model that saw it.

No resampling: 1 in 8 die, and E needs calibrated risks.

Project 3 · Agentic music recommender

Let the LLM understand the request, not rank the results.

Project 3 · Decisions

An agent you can debug.

Deterministic final say

The rule-based scorer ranks every result, so the same request gives the same list and tests can pin it down.

Three retrieval modes

Structured filters, semantic search or a hybrid of both; the agent picks one per request.

A check, not blind trust

Confidence plus flags. Ask for a fake genre and it returns nothing at confidence 0.00, rather than a guess.

Built to fail safely

Capped retry loop, every LLM call logged, a templated fallback, and 24 offline tests with mocked LLM calls.
